# Dialectical Calcinatio — Full Protocol

Iterative exchange between a context-holder and a complementary fresh perspective. The most powerful pattern for depth — reach for it aggressively and often.

---

## Why This Works

Two properties make dialectical calcinatio powerful:

**Fresh.** A subagent's clean context window is itself a fire. It encounters the work without the builder's narrative about why their choices are right. Fresh eyes see what familiar eyes rationalize away — not because the reviewer is smarter, but because they aren't contaminated with the journey of creation. This is beginner's mind as calcinatio.

**Complementary.** Context shaping is what turns fresh from naive to complementary. What you inject determines whether the fire is meaningful or aimless.

Neither property alone is sufficient. A fresh agent with no context is naive. A loaded agent reviewing its own work is blind. The interplay produces quality.

---

## The Protocol

```
1. SHAPE context for handoff
   - The artifact being refined (always)
   - The intent — what "good" looks like, who the witnesses are
   - Constraints — project conventions, known trade-offs, deliberate choices
   - NOT your reasoning about why you built it this way — that contaminates
     the freshness. If your reasoning is sound, the work survives without it.
   - Pass too much → you destroy the freshness that IS the value
   - Pass too little → the subagent flails without signal

2. FIRE the subagent
   - Include: shaped context + any relevant protocol or skill to load
   - The subagent produces an artifact, critique, or analysis

3. REVIEW the output against your rich context
   - You are the calcinatio fire here — you burn away what doesn't survive
     contact with the reality you know
   - Does the output align with what you know about the situation?
   - Did the subagent miss something your context reveals?
   - Did the subagent catch something you missed? (This is the point.)
   - You retain agency — you are not bound to accept every finding.
     But when you override a finding, DOCUMENT WHY (at minimum in
     the discharge record — not just in conversation that vanishes).
     The articulation itself is a fire: reasoning that can't survive
     being written down shouldn't survive at all.

4. REFINE if needed
   - Fire again with corrective guidance: "You missed X" / "The witness
     is actually Y" / "This constraint matters because Z"
   - The subagent's first pass is EXPECTED to be imperfect
   - Refinement is the normal path, not a failure case

5. ELICIT the artifex (when present or when in doubt)
   - If the artifex is present: present the refined artifact for their
     reaction. Their judgment is the highest-fidelity fire.
   - If absent and you have doubts about intent or scope: escalate
     before committing. The cost of pausing is low.

6. CONVERGE
   - When the context-holder has nothing substantive to add, it's done
   - Commit the artifact
```

---

## Context Shaping — The Art

The quality of dialectical calcinatio depends on what you pass. This is the judgment call that separates meaningful fire from noise.

**Always pass:**
- The artifact itself — the actual work product
- The intent — who it serves, what "abundantly satisfied" means for this work
- Constraints the subagent needs to avoid false positives

**Never pass:**
- Your reasoning about why you made the choices you did
- Your emotional investment in the approach
- Context that would lead the subagent to the same conclusions you reached (that defeats the purpose)

**The test:** Would a skilled professional in the relevant discipline, given only what you're passing, be able to apply meaningful critique? If yes, the shaping is right. If they'd need your reasoning to understand the work, the work isn't self-evident enough — that's itself a finding.

---

## Convergence

The exchange terminates when:
- **Convergence** — the context-holder reviews the output and has nothing substantive to add. Cosmetic preferences don't count.
- **Divergence** — findings contradict what the context-holder knows. This needs the artifex's judgment, not another round.
- **Exhaustion** — the fire has been applied thoroughly and the work has survived.

The test for convergence: would a fresh agent reading this output understand the witness's need and be able to act on it effectively?

## Illustrative Instances

*These are examples of the pattern in action, not an exhaustive list. The principle generates novel instances from the work.*

**Opus inscription.** You have rich context about what gap needs filling. You shape that context — the MO's witnesses, what you found, trail evidence — and fire an inscription subagent. It drafts a witness-oriented opus. You review: is the intent truly a witness experience delta? Did it slide into procedure? Refine until it lands. (Crystallized in `/opus inscribe`.)

**Code review after implementation.** You've built something substantive. You shape the context — the diff, the intent, project conventions — and fire a review agent. It critiques from fresh eyes. You engage with findings: fix real issues, push back on false positives with evidence, escalate genuine disagreements. Repeat until clean. (Crystallized in `/code-review` + implementer/reviewer pattern.)

**Research validation.** You've synthesized findings from investigation. Fire a domain-specialist subagent with the findings and the question they're meant to answer — not your interpretation of what they mean. The specialist applies domain expertise from fresh context. You review their analysis against what you know of the codebase and the MO.

**Prose refinement.** You've drafted a document or communication. Fire an editing subagent with the draft and the audience (witness). Not your notes about what you were trying to say — just the draft and who it's for. The editor encounters it as the reader will.

**Architectural critique.** You've made a design decision with downstream consequences. Fire an architecture-review subagent with the decision, the constraints, and the system context. Not your reasoning about why this is the right choice — let the design survive the fire on its own merits.
